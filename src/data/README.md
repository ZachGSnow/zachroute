# Route data

Generated by the scripts in `scripts/` (`npm run data`); do not edit by hand. Route corrections go
in `scripts/route_overrides.json`.

| File | Contents | Built from | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `us-routes.json` | US routes (including US territories) and the airlines flying them, by month | US DOT Bureau of Transportation Statistics on-time data | Public domain (US government) |
| `us-intl-routes.json` | Current nonstops between US and foreign airports (at least 8 flights in some month of the last 12), with the airlines flying them and airline names | US DOT Bureau of Transportation Statistics T-100 Segment (all carriers), `scripts/build_t100.js` | Public domain (US government) |
| `carriers.json` | Airlines per route, for the routes in `us-routes.json` and `us-intl-routes.json` (with US DOT names for airlines the app has no short name for) | US DOT Bureau of Transportation Statistics on-time data and T-100 | Public domain (US government) |
| `connections.json` | Every known nonstop airport pair | `us-routes.json` for US routes, `us-intl-routes.json` for routes between US and foreign airports, [OpenFlights](https://openflights.org/data.php) `routes.dat` for routes between two foreign airports | [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) |

`connections.json` contains information from the OpenFlights route database, which is made
available under the ODbL 1.0. As a database derived from it, `connections.json` is also made
available under the ODbL 1.0: you may share and adapt it, as long as you credit OpenFlights and
keep any adapted version of the database under the same license. The rest of this repository is
MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`).
