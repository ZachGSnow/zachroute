    ZachRoute

    Instant, offline-capable airport lookup and route planner for anywhere in the world. Search by code, city or name, then see live weather and a plain-English forecast, runways and approaches, delays, and hazards from thunderstorms and turbulence to volcanic ash, tropical cyclones, fires, smoke and dust. It's a companion app to my FlightWall setup.

    For information only. Not for navigation or flight planning. Always use official aviation sources.

    Data sources

    • Weather, forecasts, pilot reports, AIRMETs and SIGMETs worldwide: Aviation Weather Center (updated within minutes)
    • US delays: FAA; US weather alerts: National Weather Service
    • Tropical cyclones: GDACS (UN / European Commission), from the official advisories, every 6 hours
    • Fires: NASA FIRMS (last 24 hours); dust and smoke: NASA GIBS, OMPS on NOAA-20 (daily)
    • Air quality and winds aloft: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), model estimates; air quality contains Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information
    • Airports and runways: OurAirports (public domain); airlines and US routes: U.S. DOT BTS; other routes: OpenFlights (ODbL, 2017)
    • Airport history and photos: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA); magnetic variation: World Magnetic Model; map: Stadia Maps, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap

    An airport card loads its data when you search and does not refresh while open; search again for newer data.
