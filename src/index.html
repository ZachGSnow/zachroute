    ZachRoute

    Instant, offline-capable airport lookup and route planner for anywhere in the world. Search by code, city or name, then see live weather and a plain-English forecast, runways and approaches, delays, and hazards from thunderstorms and turbulence to volcanic ash, tropical cyclones, fires, smoke and dust. It's a companion app to my FlightWall setup.

    For information only. Not for navigation or flight planning. Always use official aviation sources.

    Data sources

    Weather

    • Current weather, forecasts, pilot reports, AIRMETs, SIGMETs and the thunderstorm forecast: Aviation Weather Center (public domain)
    • US weather alerts and forecaster notes: National Weather Service; severe storm outlook: NOAA Storm Prediction Center
    • US radar and storm tops: NEXRAD via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet; radar elsewhere: RainViewer (personal use, with credit)
    • Satellite clouds: NASA GIBS (NOAA GOES, JMA Himawari) and EUMETSAT (Meteosat); dust and smoke: NASA GIBS (OMPS on NOAA-20)
    • Winds aloft, freezing level, terrain height and air quality model: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0; air quality contains Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information); measured PM2.5: OpenAQ and the station's provider

    Hazards

    • US delays: FAA NAS Status; flight restrictions: FAA TFRs
    • Tropical cyclones: National Hurricane Center (Atlantic, East and Central Pacific) and GDACS (UN / European Commission) elsewhere
    • Earthquakes: USGS; US volcano alerts: USGS Volcano Hazards Program
    • Fires: NASA FIRMS (last 24 hours); space weather and aurora: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center

    Airspace and charts

    • US airspace: FAA open data; charts: FAA Aeronautical Information Services
    • Airspace elsewhere: openAIP (CC BY-NC 4.0)

    Airports, routes and traffic

    • Airports, runways, frequencies and navaids: OurAirports (public domain); magnetic variation: World Magnetic Model
    • US routes, airlines and traffic: U.S. DOT Bureau of Transportation Statistics (on-time data and T-100); routes between two foreign airports: OpenFlights (ODbL, 2017)
    • History and photos: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), matched through Wikidata (CC0)

    Map and terrain

    • Maps: Stadia Maps, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap contributors, Stamen Design
    • Live track aircraft and flight routes: adsb.lol contributors (ODbL 1.0), routes from the Virtual Radar Server standing data
    • Satellite base map: EOxCloudless 2025 by EOX IT Services GmbH, contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
    • Terrain shading: AWS Open Data terrain tiles (Mapzen, from SRTM, ETOPO1, USGS and others)
